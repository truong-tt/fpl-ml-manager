# Fantasy Premier League (FPL) 101

This document explains the basic rules and mechanics of the official Fantasy Premier League (FPL) game. If you are reviewing the Machine Learning architecture in this repository and are unfamiliar with soccer or fantasy sports, this guide provides the necessary domain context.

## What is FPL?
FPL is a game where you act as the manager of a virtual soccer team made up of real-life players from the English Premier League (EPL). Your virtual players score points based on their actual real-world performances in EPL matches each week.

The English Premier League season consists of 38 "Gameweeks" (rounds of matches). The goal is to accumulate the most points over the 38 Gameweeks.

---

## 1. Squad Selection & Budget Constraints
You must build a squad of **15 players** under strict constraints:
* **Budget:** You have a virtual budget of **£100.0 million**. Player prices range from £4.0m (bench players) to £15.0m (superstars).
* **Positions:** Your 15-man squad must consist exactly of:
  * 2 Goalkeepers (GK)
  * 5 Defenders (DEF)
  * 5 Midfielders (MID)
  * 3 Forwards (FWD)
* **Team Limits:** You can select a maximum of **3 players** from any single real-world team (e.g., you cannot have 4 Arsenal players).

## 2. The Starting XI & Substitutes
Before each Gameweek deadline, you must choose **11 starters** from your 15-man squad. 
* Only the 11 starters will score points for your team.
* The remaining 4 players sit on your "Bench."
* **Valid Formations:** Your starting 11 must include at least 1 Goalkeeper, 3 Defenders, and 1 Forward.
* **Auto-Substitutions:** If one of your starting 11 players does not play a single minute in real life, the game automatically swaps them with the highest-priority player on your bench who *did* play (provided it doesn't break the valid formation rules).

## 3. Captaincy
You must choose one player in your starting 11 to be your **Captain** and one to be your **Vice-Captain**.
* The **Captain's** points are doubled for that Gameweek.
* If your Captain plays 0 minutes in real life, the double points automatically pass to your Vice-Captain. 

## 4. How Players Score Points
Points are awarded based on real-world match events. The scoring heavily depends on the player's position:

**Playing Time:**
* Playing up to 60 minutes: **1 point**
* Playing 60 minutes or more: **2 points**

**Attacking Returns:**
* **Goals:** Goalkeepers get **10 points**, Defenders get **6 points**, Midfielders get **5 points**, Forwards get **4 points**.
* **Assists:** All positions get **3 points** for an assist. (Note: As of 25/26, assist rules have been simplified to award more assists from deflections, woodwork rebounds, and inadvertent touches).

**Defensive Returns & Contributions (Clean Sheets & CBIT/CBIRT):**
* **Clean Sheets:** A "Clean Sheet" means the player's team did not concede any goals while they were on the pitch (must play at least 60 mins to qualify). Goalkeepers/Defenders get **4 points**. Midfielders get **1 point**.
* **Defensive Actions:** Under the new 25/26 rules, outfield players are rewarded for defensive work:
  * Defenders get **2 points** for accumulating 10 combined Clearances, Blocks, Interceptions, and Tackles (CBIT).
  * Midfielders/Forwards get **2 points** for accumulating 12 combined Clearances, Blocks, Interceptions, Tackles, and ball Recoveries (CBIRT).

**Negative Points:**
* For every 2 goals conceded (Defenders/Goalkeepers): **-1 point**
* Yellow Card: **-1 point**
* Red Card: **-3 points**
* Missed Penalty: **-2 points**
* Own Goal: **-2 points**

**Bonus Points System (BPS):**
At the end of every match, the 3 best-performing players (determined by an underlying statistical matrix of passes, tackles, shots, etc.) are awarded bonus points:
* Best player: **3 points**
* Second best: **2 points**
* Third best: **1 point**
*(Note: 25/26 rule tweaks heavily increased BPS for Goal-line clearances to 9 BPS, alongside adjustments for saves and successful tackles).*

## 5. Transfers & Chips (The Market)
Because players get injured, lose form, or face difficult upcoming schedules, you must make changes to your squad week-to-week.
* **Free Transfers:** You receive **1 Free Transfer** per Gameweek. You can bank and save up to **5 free transfers** at a time. In Gameweek 16, managers are automatically topped up to the maximum 5 transfers to plan around the Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON).
* **Points Penalty (Hits):** If you want to make more transfers than your free allocation, it costs **-4 points** per extra transfer. This penalty is deducted directly from your overall score.
* **Price Changes:** Player prices rise and fall by £0.1m increments based on how many real-world FPL managers are buying or selling them. 

**Chips (New 25/26 Structure):**
Managers now receive **TWO sets of chips** per season (one set for the first half, and a refreshed set for the second half). Each set contains:
* **Wildcard:** Unlimited free transfers.
* **Free Hit:** Unlimited free transfers for one week only (your squad reverts to its previous state at the next deadline).
* **Triple Captain:** Your Captain's points are tripled instead of doubled.
* **Bench Boost:** Points from your 4 benched players are included in your Gameweek total.

*(Note: The first set of 4 chips must be used before the Gameweek 19 deadline or they will expire. Playing a Wildcard or Free Hit no longer resets your banked free transfers. The core ML agent currently focuses on the standard weekly transfer market).*
